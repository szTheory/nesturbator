## Strategic Playstead direction

The owner has clarified that getting the first emulator working reliably end to end through libfuturo is strategically important for Playstead. The likely first-route candidates are Nesturbator (NES) and Muhtari (Atari 7800); prioritize whichever can meet the real Playstead acceptance bar first. Playstead currently supports libretro as a working bridge and should move qualified routes through a strangler migration toward libfuturo as soon as they are ready. This is a priority, not a completed cutover or a selection of Nesturbator over Muhtari.

That makes route qualification a serious next-scope candidate alongside conditional RM-015. Nesturbator has a useful head start: an older installed NTSC consumer already exercises software A/V and battery save/restart. It merits a bounded current-source requalification comparison first, but that does not prove it is the fastest route. Muhtari has more current libfuturo native-host work, while its selected host slice is silent. Compare the remaining gates against the same Playstead acceptance bar before choosing. Keep Nesturbator's milestone limited to upstream source/package work it can own; libfuturo host/runtime and Playstead adoption remain in their project workflows.

## Conditional core-accuracy option

The current Nesturbator roadmap keeps RM-015 conditional on bounded native/source triage. Candidate observations include Jurassic Park's OCEAN wobble, Marble Madness' text box, Crystalis' moving seam, and reported Mega Man 3 seams. They are investigation leads, not diagnoses. Before promotion, separate core output from adapter conversion, host presentation, setup/input, and original game behavior. Require a repeatable current-source reproduction plus an independent, relevant oracle. A screenshot, same-emulator replay, or another emulator's output alone does not establish the expected result.

If a core fault is confirmed, prefer one narrow correction and an owned regression fixture that distinguishes the known-wrong model from the corrected one. Keep commercial content, private captures, and saves out of Git; use original/owned fixtures for CI. Preserve clean-room restrictions and do not inspect GPL/LGPL emulator implementations. If the symptom is host-only, expected original behavior, or remains unlocalized after bounded triage, record that and skip RM-015.

Given the new route priority, compare a Nesturbator libfuturo version-requalification slice with RM-015 on the user's expected Playstead value and remaining evidence. Do not automatically choose the route just because the current checkout is Nesturbator. If neither Nesturbator route work nor RM-015 has its required evidence, scoped maintenance remains the smaller fallback. Dendy needs its own timing evidence and demand. MMC2/Punch-Out!! and measured performance remain Mid-horizon proposals; selected expansion audio, MMC5, FDS, and peripherals remain Long-horizon proposals.
