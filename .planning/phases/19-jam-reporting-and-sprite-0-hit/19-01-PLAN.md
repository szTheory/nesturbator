- Macros `ACCURACYCOIN_ACTIVE_RUNNING` (0), `ACCURACYCOIN_ACTIVE_BUDGET` (1), `ACCURACYCOIN_ACTIVE_JAM` (2) in `runner/accuracycoin.h`; the `int active_budget_expired` parameter of `accuracycoin_classify` / `accuracycoin_classify_row` is renamed `active_state` (same `int` type, ABI-neutral for callers passing 0/1).

- Report fixture mutations `jam` (accepted), `jam-cause-dropped`, `jam-row-dropped`, `jam-frame-missing` (rejected).
- `accuracycoin.tracer` assertion `jam_frame=-`.

## Edge coverage and flagged assumptions

- JAM-01 (unclassified probe row, unresolved): flagged assumption. The edge cases this plan pins explicitly are: JAM on the execution stage with an attributable row; JAM before execution (boot stage via reset-vector patch); JAM in strict mode; JAM while no TEST row is current (attribution `-`). A JAM landing on the exact cap frame is not separately constructed; the JAM check precedes the cap chain, so a JAM always wins over a budget timeout — recorded as an assumption for the verifier.
