## Decisions taken under Claude's discretion

- **Exit codes:** a reported blargg result 1-0x7F exits with that value; 120 timeout, 121 no signature, 122 load failure, 123 usage. 77 is never used. `oracle.txt`'s `code` column holds the expected exit status as `0xNN` (pass rows `0x00`).
- **Keys:** `mmc3_test_2/<name>` and `mmc3_irq_tests/<name>` without `.nes`; test names `mmc3.oracle.<key>`.
- **Sizes:** taken with `file(SIZE)` after checkout, or from research section 5 (40976 and 16400), never with a blob-reading git command in the blobless tree (research pitfall 9).
- **Judgement in a header** so Plan 11's `mmc3.oracle.unit` proves pass, timeout and no-signature offline on synthetic memory, without the network.
- **Split from the measurement work (checker revision):** this plan carries the lane, its fetch guard and its docs; Plan 11 carries the 11 further rows, the RESEARCH open question 1 rule and the offline checks, so each plan stays at or below 10 files.
