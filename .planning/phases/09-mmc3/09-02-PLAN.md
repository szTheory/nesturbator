## Decisions taken under Claude's discretion

- **Seed split (checker revision):** D-16's 31 seeds are committed over four plans so no plan modifies 15 or more files: the five valid seeds here, then the reject seeds in Plans 04 (submapper, four-screen, mappers 118 and 119), 05 (mappers 206 and 249, PRG and CHR-ROM sizes) and 06 (CHR-RAM, CHR-NVRAM and RAM shapes). Every D-16 row is written here so the loader rule is pinned in one place; each later plan proves its seeds against these rows by name.
- **Seed builder:** a throwaway scratchpad script, never committed (CLAUDE.md rule 3), writes a header, zeros, a reset vector to `$8000` and `4C 00 80`. Later seed plans recreate it if the scratchpad is gone.
- **Seed pin:** each manifest line uses `git rev-parse HEAD` at creation time, in the format of the existing seed lines.
- **Re-tagging (D-16):** research section 8 scanned every `NES\x1a` seed with the loader's NES 2.0 bit rule and found none decoding to mapper 4; Task 3 repeats the scan as a check and records the result in the summary.
