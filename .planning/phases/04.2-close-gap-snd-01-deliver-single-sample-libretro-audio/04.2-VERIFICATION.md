## Goal Achievement

### Observable Truths

| # | Truth | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A sample-only frontend receives ordered stereo pairs for each produced core PCM sample. | ✓ VERIFIED | `retro_run` iterates `i` from zero through `io.audio_count - 1`, duplicates `mono[i]` into both `stereo[2*i]` channels, and calls `audio_cb` once per pair when the batch callback is absent. `check_sound_frame_parity` compares every pair and count against an independently stepped core for two audible frames. |

| 3 | Empty successful frames and failed frames deliver no audio callbacks. | ✓ VERIFIED | A zero sample count skips dispatch. | 4 | The libretro host test runs deterministically in CI without audio hardware. | ✓ VERIFIED | `libretro.host` is registered in `tests/CMakeLists.txt`; this follow-up run of `cmake --workflow --preset ci` passed all 357 tests, including the new failed-frame assertions. |
