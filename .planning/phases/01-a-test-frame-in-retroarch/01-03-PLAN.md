   - Signal levels in mV: low 228 312 552 880; high 616 840 1100 1100; attenuated low 192 256 448 712; attenuated high 500 676 896 896.
   - Colour $xY (hue Y = entry & 15, level = (entry >> 4) & 3) is high on phase p (0–11) when (Y + p) mod 12 < 6. Hue 0 is high only; hues 13–15 are low only. $xE/$xF force the black level (level-1 low, 312 mV).
   - Emphasis: native bit 6 = PPUMASK bit 5 → wave 0xC; native bit 7 = PPUMASK bit 6 → wave 4; native bit 8 = PPUMASK bit 7 → wave 8. On phases where an emphasised wave is high, use the attenuated level. $xE/$xF are excepted. The emphasis table is from the "Colour emphasis" rev 23220 and "PPU palettes" rev 24257 pages; the mapping matches D-13.
   - Normalise (v − 312) / (1100 − 312). Y is the mean over 12 phases. U and V are demodulated with chroma gain 2 at reference angles 15° + 30°·k. Their sines and cosines are ±(√6−√2)/4, ±√2/2 and ±(√6+√2)/4, computed with `sqrt` only, so `cos`, `sin` and `pow` are never used.
   - The matrix comes from 0.299/0.587/0.114 with 0.492111 (B−Y) and 0.877283 (R−Y). Clip each channel to [0,1]. Round with `(uint32_t)(x * 255.0 + 0.5)`.
