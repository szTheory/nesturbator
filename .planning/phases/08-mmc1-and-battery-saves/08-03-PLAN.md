## Decisions taken under Claude's discretion

- **Row reading is a decoder mode, not a second program** (D-03 discretion): `--prg-ram` prints the text and the CTest asserts it with a quote-anchored regex, so `32K PRG RAM OK` cannot match `32K PRG RAM OK + BATTERY` (research Pattern 6).
- **The fourth field is required for every entry,** including m2, m3 and m7, so all eight ROMs assert their PRG RAM row. Those three have no PRG-RAM (byte 10 = 0), and research's reading of Holy Mapperel `src/main.s` gives `PRG RAM MISSING` with no number.
- **The decoder lands before the MMC1 ROMs** so its tracer runs on ROMs already in the tree and needs no Phase 8 core change; the `:save` field is parsed here and used first in Plan 07.
- **This plan runs in wave 2 after Plan 01** only because both edit `tests/CMakeLists.txt`.
