ROM admission (files, manifest lines, notices) stays in Task 1 with the decoder because the hygiene hook requires the ROMs and their manifest lines in one commit, and the tracer needs a committed ROM to prove the dump-decode chain end to end. - **One hash row per ROM at frame N, stability checked at run time (D-08):** `write_hashes.cmake` hashes frame N and frame 2N, fails if they differ, and writes only the frame N row. No audio rows: the beeps are not under test (research Pitfall 6).
